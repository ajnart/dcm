# Owned-content boundary

Use this stack only for media the user owns, created, licensed, may lawfully
download, or that is in the public domain. Confirm the content source and rights
before configuring an acquisition path or starting a real request.

- Support organization, streaming, indexing, metadata, transcoding, and backup
  of authorized media.
- Keep qBittorrent and indexer guidance source-neutral. A technically working
  provider does not establish permission to acquire its content.
- Avoid instructions that bypass payment, access controls, DRM, territorial
  restrictions, or copyright enforcement.
- Decline acquisition automation when the stated purpose is unauthorized
  copying. Offer lawful sources, library workflows, or owned-media ingestion
  instead.
- Keep acceptance read-only: search may prove connectivity, while a request or
  download requires separate explicit authorization for a lawful item.

The user remains responsible for licenses, local law, retention, sharing, and
network-provider terms. Record the authorization boundary in the deployment
plan without collecting proof of purchase or other sensitive documents.
