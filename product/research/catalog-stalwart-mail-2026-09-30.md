# Stalwart Mail shared setup decision — 2026-09-30

Decision: keep `stalwart-mail` discovery-only. Do not publish `recipe.json` or `setup.ts` as an installer or deterministic first-admin route. The existing pinned image record is useful for planning, but a private HTTP bootstrap container is not an operational mail server.

[Official Docker installation](https://stalw.art/docs/install/platform/docker/) requires separate persistent `/etc/stalwart` and `/var/lib/stalwart` volumes. Its example publishes management, SMTP, IMAP, POP3, and ManageSieve ports, although individual ports may be omitted. The image runs as UID 2000 without extra capabilities. A loopback-only 8080 recipe would safely expose the bootstrap UI, but would not fulfill the app's mail-server role.

[Bootstrap mode](https://stalw.art/docs/configuration/bootstrap-mode/) starts no mail services and exposes only HTTP 8080 until `config.json` exists. It prints a one-time temporary `admin` password. `STALWART_RECOVERY_ADMIN` can pin that credential, but is a recovery login, not the permanent administrator. The wizard collects hostname, domain, stores, and directory choices, creates the permanent administrator, and requires a restart. Normal administration then moves to HTTPS at the configured hostname. Keeping a generated `STALWART_RECOVERY_ADMIN` in a persistent Compose recipe would retain a standing recovery credential; the current first-run contract cannot remove it and verify that removal as part of a staged owner revision.

The official [declarative deployment](https://stalw.art/docs/configuration/declarative-deployments/) alternative templates `config.json`, starts with recovery mode and a secret, runs `stalwart-cli apply`, and restarts without the recovery variables. This needs an approved, staged configuration and secret lifecycle, exact domain and TLS choices, and authenticated post-restart verification. The shared recipe/schema and adapter contract currently describe a single Compose revision plus first-run actions, not that transition. An [API key](https://stalw.art/docs/auth/authentication/api-key/) is a separate management credential; it does not substitute for the permanent administrator or mail-protocol acceptance.

Promotion requires a safe staged bootstrap/declarative path, permanent-admin login and recovery-login removal proof, then separately approved public port ownership, DNS and TLS, outbound and inbound mail round trips, anti-abuse controls, backup restore, and restart persistence. No container, mail, DNS, or TLS validation was performed in this source-only review.
