# Frigate catalog decision (2026-09-30)

Decision: retain guided support. Do not add a shared Compose recipe or `setup.ts` yet. The catalog can identify a pinned image, but the shared setup contract cannot complete a camera NVR deployment from non-secret inputs alone.

- Upstream [v0.18.0 release](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/releases/tag/v0.18.0) lists `ghcr.io/blakeblackshear/frigate:0.18.0` and architecture/hardware-specific variants. The [starter Compose](https://docs.frigate.video/guides/getting_started/) uses mutable `:stable`, `/config`, `/media/frigate`, a 1 GB `/tmp/cache` tmpfs, authenticated HTTPS port 8971 and RTSP port 8554. It explicitly says the sample is amd64-only and must be customized.
- [Camera setup](https://docs.frigate.video/guides/getting_started/) is a separate operation: select an actual camera stream, provide its RTSP URL/credentials privately, choose detect/record roles, and verify decoded video. Recording retention and disk sizing are operator-specific. The recommended accelerator mapping is host-specific (`/dev/dri`, Coral USB/PCIe); no device or privileged mode should be inferred. The default CPU detector does not prove the host can sustain the selected streams.
- [Authentication](https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/authentication/) generates the initial `admin` password in container logs, recommends rotating it, and distinguishes authenticated port 8971 from unauthenticated port 5000. The latter must not be published. The documented [`POST /api/login`](https://docs.frigate.video/integrations/api/login-login-post/) and authenticated `/api/profile` can verify a captured credential. [`GET /api/`](https://docs.frigate.video/integrations/api/is-healthy-get/) is a public health endpoint, but a 200 response does not verify camera streaming, detection, recording, or restart persistence.

Exact gap: the shared recipe's current static non-secret configuration file and onboarding forms cannot accept a user-selected camera configuration with private RTSP credentials, inspect its stream, safely capture the generated first password from the exact container startup without exposing logs, rotate and verify it, and then verify one actual camera plus recordings. A dashboard-only recipe would overstate completion. A future adapter needs those bounded inputs and checks, or a separately approved guided install can perform them for a specific host.
